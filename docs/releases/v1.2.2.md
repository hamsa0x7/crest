**New**

- A LeetCode tool: problems solved by difficulty, your current and longest streak and the last 36 weeks of submissions, from a public username with no sign-in. A part in the closed notch shows today's count. Turn the tool on under Settings → Tools and enter the username under Settings → Connections ([#47](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/47)).
- **Keep scrolling**, beside the song title's Lyrics switch in Settings → Display: a title too long for its part keeps scrolling while the song plays, instead of stopping after three laps ([#53](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/53)).
- Two switches for the open panel, both on by default. Turning off **Resize by dragging the edges** (Settings → Window) takes the grips off the panel's sides and bottom; turning off **Hovering a part opens its tool** (Settings → General) lets the notch open as usual under a part of the closed row, while a click on a part still opens its tool ([#50](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/50)).

**Fixes**

- On Macs with Apple silicon, macOS no longer calls the downloaded app damaged: the app is now signed ad hoc, so macOS asks once to allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway ([#52](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/52)).
- A lyric line too long for the closed notch scrolls once and stays at its end until the next line, instead of going back to its start ([#54](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/54)).
- With **Hide the Windows indicator** on, Windows' volume and brightness indicator shows while a window rule or the hide shortcut hides Crest, instead of no indicator at all ([#50](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/50)).
- A click on a system notification or a tray icon's notice, which Windows cannot open by its app id, opens the Notices page instead of the Documents folder ([#50](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/50)).

Try every tool in your browser before installing: [crestisland.app](https://crestisland.app/).
